# 0002. VLM v0.1: the interfaces that are now frozen

- **Status:** proposed for VLM v0.1 (Venti plan T57.3), 2026-10-16; accepted
  when the owner merges it and tags `v0.1.0`
- **Context:** ADR 0001 listed what would be frozen at v0.1 and what stays
  draft. VLM v0.1 is validated (Venti T57: LA passes the VLM gate of decision
  0003; Houston is an edge case). Frozen means: fields and layers are added,
  never renamed or removed, and a change of meaning needs a new decision.

## Frozen

1. **Runconfig** (`opera_vlm_workflow`), groups and field names:
   - `input_file_group`: `frame_id`, `ascending_disp_files`,
     `descending_disp_files`, `ascending_cal_files`, `descending_cal_files`,
     `unr_grid_latlon_file`, `unr_timeseries_dir`;
   - `dynamic_ancillary_file_group`: `algorithm_parameters_file`,
     `los_file_ascending`, `los_file_descending`;
   - `static_ancillary_file_group`: `algorithm_parameters_overrides_json`,
     `defo_area_db_json`, `gia_model_file`;
   - `primary_executable`, `output_options`, `product_path_group`,
     `worker_settings`, `log_file`, `run_input_prechecks`.
2. **Product layers:** `vertical`, `vertical_std`, `east`, `east_std`,
   `mode` (meters; `mode` 0 none, 1 asc+desc WLS, 2 single-geometry
   projection), and the context layers `plate_motion_east_rate`,
   `plate_motion_north_rate` (always) and `gia_vertical_rate` (with
   `gia_model_file`), in meters per year, never removed from `vertical` or
   `east`.
3. **Groups:** `/identification` and `/metadata`, including the GNSS
   provenance record.
4. **One product per target pair `(T0, Tk)`**, all sharing `T0` (ADR 0001
   decision 4).
5. **The GNSS realisation must match DISP-CAL's** (provenance check, error
   1003). With DISP-CAL made with remove-restore (cal-disp v0.5.0 and later)
   the VLM run gives the same `gnss_options` (`buffer_meters`,
   `exclude_defo_nodes`, `reinterpolate_excluded`) and the same
   `defo_area_db_json` (see [Running on cal-disp v0.5 inputs](../v05-inputs.md)).
6. **Exit and error codes** in `docs/error-codes.md` are never reused or
   renumbered.

## Still draft (may change in a minor release, with a CHANGELOG entry)

- Algorithm parameters: `decomposition_options` (`cond_max`, `min_up`,
  `sigma_disp_meters`, `sigma_rate_meters_per_year`) and `temporal_options`
  (window, target spacing). Studies may move them; defaults are recorded per
  release.
- The velocity-domain product (`output_options.write_velocity_product`, not
  implemented).
- **Tiling (Venti plan T63, owner 2026-10-16):** the VLM SAS will produce per
  MGRS tile from every overlapping asc/desc frame. That adds fields (tile id,
  tile database, frame lists) to the runconfig; under this record it may not
  rename or remove the frozen ones above.

## Evidence

disp2vlm_validation `docs/vnv/vlm-v0.1-report.md`: LA (F16940 + F18904) passes
the VLM gate (172 stations, median −0.42, NMAD 1.44 mm/yr); Houston (F08882 +
F38238) is not assessable (19 gated stations) and stays an edge case.
